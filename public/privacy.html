Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2025

Hangout App ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Account Information: Name, email address, date of birth, gender
  • Profile Information: Profile picture, Instagram username (optional)
  • Location Data: Your current location to show nearby activities

Activity Information

  • Activities you create or join
  • Messages sent in group chats
  • Images shared in conversations

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Show you nearby activities based on your location
  • Enable communication with other users in activities
  • Send notifications about activities and messages
  • Ensure the safety and security of our platform

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only in these circumstances:

  • With Other Users: Your profile information is visible to users in activities you join
  • Service Providers: We use Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, and storage
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect rights and safety

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication through Firebase
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and data isolation

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and data
  • Opt-out of location tracking (this may limit app functionality)
  • Export your data

6. Location Data

We collect and use your location data to:

  • Show activities near you
  • Allow you to create location-based activities
  • Calculate distances to activities

You can disable location services in your device settings, but this will limit core app functionality.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data: Until you delete your account
  • Activity data: Activities expire after their scheduled time
  • Messages: Retained while the activity is active
  • Images: Stored until manually deleted

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

9. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, Database, Storage
  • Google Maps: Location services and mapping

These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending you a notification through the app
